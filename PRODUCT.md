# Product

## Register

brand

(Primary public surface `/` — the live wedding photo wall — is a brand/emotional surface. `/admin` is product register: utilitarian, fast, no ceremony.)

## Users

- Wedding guests (~40-50, RU/KZ mix, ~20 kids, many older relatives): glance at the wall on phones between tasks, outdoors/indoor evening light. Play happens in Telegram; the site is for watching the day accumulate.
- The venue TV: the wall runs full-screen across the room — must read from meters away.
- Ilya & Aigul (admins): manage guests/tasks/submissions from a phone during their own wedding. Seconds matter.
- After the day: the wall becomes the permanent photo archive the couple shares.

## Product Purpose

Live photo wall + wishes + game leaderboard for Ilya & Aigul's August 2026 wedding. Success: guests see their photos appear within seconds of submitting, wishes scroll by, leaderboard fuels friendly competition, and the finished page is a keepsake.

## Brand Personality

Celebratory, warm, personal. A wedding evening — not an app. Three words: festive, intimate, alive. The photos ARE the design; chrome recedes.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS dashboard aesthetics (metric cards, gradient accents).
- Wedding-template kitsch: script fonts everywhere, gold flourishes, pastel rose gradients.
- Cream/beige "elegant" AI default backgrounds.

## Design Principles

1. Photos first — the grid is the hero; UI elements never compete with guest photos.
2. Readable across the room — TV mode means big type, high contrast, motion that reads at distance.
3. Trilingual is first-class — RU/EN/KK switch is instant, no layout breakage in any language.
4. Alive — new photos/wishes arrive with gentle motion; the page visibly breathes during the event.
5. Admin is boring on purpose — fast forms, big tap targets, zero decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Older guests: body text ≥16px on phones, contrast ≥4.5:1 everywhere.
- prefers-reduced-motion honored (crossfade instead of movement).
- Works on cheap Android phones and slow venue Wi-Fi (compressed images, no heavy libs).
